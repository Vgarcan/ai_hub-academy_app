How AI Hub works

Intelligence that stays
visible at every step.

AI Hub runs intelligent workflows that can understand a goal, select tools, retain context, validate outcomes and produce an auditable result — all in a structure you can inspect.

  • Every workflow step is logged, inspectable and traceable
  • Swap AI providers without changing how the workflow runs
  • Memory and context carry forward between steps, not just within a single response
  • Human approval can be required before any action is taken or decision confirmed
